# Loud, Clear, and Real.

Founded in **April 2023**, **The After Class Podcast** was born out of a simple observation: the most interesting, honest, and impactful conversations students have aren't happening in lecture halls—they're happening *after class*.

## Our Mission
We provide a dedicated space for authentic student stories and conversations. We believe in being **loud** about our experiences, **clear** about our goals, and **real** about the challenges facing the Gen Z community today.

## What We Cover
- **Business & Careers:** How students are building the future.
- **Relationships:** Navigating dynamics in a modern world.
- **Mental Health:** Real talk about the pressures of campus life.
- **Lifestyle:** From car culture to creative arts.

## The Journey
Since our launch, we've grown from a local project into a recognized platform for youthful expression. Every episode is an invitation to join the crew and participate in the dialogue that defines our generation.
